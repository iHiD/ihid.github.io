Bio

Jeremy Walker is a technologist and founder. He is the CEO of Exercism, a nonprofit that has taught over 3 million people to code, and the founder of Jiki. He co-founded the employee wellbeing company Kaido and holds a patent for an AI algorithm that detects errors during interventional cardiology. He served as a Non-Executive Director of Beatfreeks from 2014 to 2019 and holds a degree in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from the University of Birmingham.
