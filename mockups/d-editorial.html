Building Exercism

For nearly a decade I have led Exercism, a nonprofit coding-education platform teaching 85 programming languages, entirely free. It is one of the world's largest open-source organisations by contributors, and we built it for under $500,000.

I set the strategy, raise the money, lead the community and write the code. I have led two full rewrites of the platform, and I built the agentic system that translates it into 19 languages.

Applied AI, since before it was fashionable

I graduated in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence from the University of Birmingham in 2005.

In 2018 I partnered with some of the world's leading cardiologists to build a deep-learning system that detects errors in real time during interventional cardiology. I built the tool the doctors used to create its training data and developed the algorithm. It outperforms doctors at error detection and is patented.

I have secured and led two Innovate UK projects, one of which built real-time NLP and recommendation algorithms across thousands of pieces of medical content. Today I build agentic systems in production at Exercism and Jiki.
