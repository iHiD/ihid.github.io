Leadership

Building Exercism

For nearly a decade I have led Exercism, a nonprofit coding-education platform teaching 85 programming languages, entirely free. We have grown to over 3 million students entirely by word of mouth, and we are one of the world's largest open-source organisations by contributors. We built it all for under $500,000.

  • Led the businessSet the strategy and raised £1.5M+ in donations from thousands of individuals and from organisations including Google, Mozilla and the University of Chicago.
  • Recruited and led the teamRecruited and retained 150 volunteer maintainers, many committing 10+ hours a week for years, alongside a small full-time staff, 5,500 code contributors and 19,000+ mentors.
  • Led technical developmentCoded hands-on and built systems that let maintainers work autonomously. We run 85 languages in production across 450+ repositories, all deploying automatically.
  • Iterated on the productLed two full rewrites, modernising the platform each time, and translated the site into 19 languages with a custom agentic system that works directly with native speakers.

Artificial intelligence

Twenty years of applied AI

I studied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Birmingham, graduating in 2005, and have applied AI throughout my career.

A patented medical-AI algorithm

In 2018 I partnered with some of the world's leading cardiologists to build an AI system that detects errors in real time during interventional cardiology. I built the tool the doctors used to create training data and developed the deep-learning algorithm. It outperforms doctors at error detection, is patented in the US, Japan and South Korea, and is being licensed for use in industry.

Two Innovate UK projects

I secured and led two UK government-backed Innovate UK projects. The first, at Meducation, built real-time NLP and recommendation algorithms across thousands of pieces of medical content. The second funded the cardiology work.

Agentic systems in production

Exercism's site is translated into 19 languages by an agentic system I built. It works directly with native speakers and updates translations in real time from their feedback.

An AI-first product build

At Jiki, an AI-first approach let a small team build technology that would previously have taken years, including custom interpreters for JavaScript and Python designed for learners.
