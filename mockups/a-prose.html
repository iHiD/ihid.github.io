I'm a technologist and founder. I lead Exercism, a nonprofit that has taught 3.1 million people to code, and I have spent twenty years building organisations at the intersection of technology, product and business.

I studied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Birmingham, and I hold a patent for a deep-learning algorithm that detects errors during interventional cardiology. I co-founded Kaido, which reached 500,000 employees at companies including HSBC, Accenture and the NHS, and I recently founded Jiki to teach complete beginners to code in the age of LLMs.

Exercism

I have been CEO of Exercism since 2017. It teaches 85 programming languages, entirely free, and has grown to over 3 million students by word of mouth alone. We have delivered more than 559 million minutes of free education, and we built the whole thing for under $500,000.

  • I set the strategy and raised £1.5M+ in donations from thousands of individuals and from organisations including Google, Mozilla and the University of Chicago.
  • I recruited and led 150 volunteer maintainers, many giving 10+ hours a week for years, alongside a small full-time staff, 5,500 code contributors and 19,000+ mentors. We are one of the world's largest open-source organisations by contributors.
  • I code hands-on. We run 85 languages in production across 450+ repositories, all deploying automatically, and I have led two full rewrites of the platform.
  • I built an agentic system that translates the site into 19 languages, working directly with native speakers and updating in real time from their feedback.

AI

In 2018 I partnered with some of the world's leading cardiologists to build an AI system that detects errors in real time during interventional cardiology. I built the tool the doctors used to create training data and developed the deep-learning algorithm. It outperforms doctors at error detection and is now patented in the US, Japan and South Korea.

I have secured and led two Innovate UK projects. The first built real-time NLP and recommendation algorithms across thousands of pieces of medical content, and the second funded the cardiology work. More recently I have built agentic systems in production at Exercism and Jiki.
